# EFF passphrase word list

`src/components/assets/eff-large-wordlist.json` is adapted from the 7,776-word
EFF Large Wordlist by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (2016).
Dice indices were removed and the list was converted to JSON. Internal hyphens
were removed from four entries (drop-down, felt-tip, t-shirt, yo-yo) so each
supported separator consistently separates the selected words. The resulting
duplicate `yoyo` was removed, leaving 7,775 unique entries.
